# Dashboard Surface Brief

## Mode

Operate.

## Approved Reference

`.impeccable/mocks/relay-dashboard-c.png`

## First Viewport

A single urgent assignment owns the top action band. Below it, tasks split into “At risk” and “On track” rows. One selected row opens its escalation ladder inline so the product's distinguishing mechanism is visible without navigating away.

## Visitor Path

Accept or decline the next action, scan risks, inspect an escalation route, then move to the full task queue or inbox.

## Signature Interaction

Selecting a task advances a thin route indicator and reveals the escalation ladder in place. State changes use a shared-axis/fade-through motion and collapse to an instant update under reduced motion.

## Cross-Surface Reach

The route grammar applies to task detail timelines, policy editing, notifications, and analytics. Lists remain rows; sheets and detail panes carry focused editing.

## Honest Risk

Dense operational rows can become illegible at large text sizes. The implementation must collapse secondary columns and allow row height to grow through 200% scaling.
